Before You Flash

  • Unlock your bootloader (this wipes data).
  • Backup all your data — internal storage will be erased.
  • Ensure battery is above 60%.
  • Verify file checksums (SHA256) after download.
  • Disclaimer: I am not responsible for bricked devices, lost data, or any damage. Proceed at your own risk.

FAQ

How do I open MEGA links?
Click "Download (MEGA)" — it opens in a new tab. For large files, MEGA may suggest their desktop app for better performance.
How do I flash a ROM zip?
Boot to custom recovery (TWRP/OrangeFox) → Wipe data/cache/dalvik → Install → Select ROM zip → Swipe to flash → Reboot.
How do I flash a kernel?
Same as ROM: boot to recovery → Install → Select kernel zip → Swipe to flash → Reboot. Check kernel-ROM compatibility.
How do I flash stock firmware (fastboot)?
Boot to fastboot (Power + Volume Down) → Connect via USB → Run flash script or flash partitions manually with fastboot flash .
What if my device won't boot?
Boot to recovery (Power + Volume Up) and restore a backup, or re-flash stock firmware via fastboot/EDL mode.
